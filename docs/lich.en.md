# Lunar calendar calculation

[Bản tiếng Việt](lich.md)

In the Chinese-style lunar calendar — more precisely, a lunisolar calendar — a **new moon** is the instant at which the new moon occurs in the local time zone. It determines the first day of a lunar month: the first day is the local civil day containing that instant, even when it occurs at 23:59:59.

A lunar month ends at 23:59:59 on the day before the next new moon. Because the Moon's mean synodic cycle is about 29.5302 days, a lunar month has either 29 or 30 days under this rounding convention. A 30-day month is a full month; a 29-day month is a short month.
